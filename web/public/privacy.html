Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The Sand Gallery is a small, private, personal project-tracking app. It has no ads and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: only your name, email address and profile picture, used to identify your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your content: the projects, notes, times, costs, photos and videos you add.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push address for your device so we can show timer notifications.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored in a private database (Supabase, EU region). Photos and videos are stored in private cloud storage (Backblaze B2, EU region). Each account can only access its own data.

If you use the offline version, nothing is sent to us: everything stays in your browser on your device.

Who can sign in

The online version is invite-only. Accounts that are not on the owner's invite list cannot see or store anything.

Deleting your data

You can export everything from Settings at any time. To have your account and all its data deleted, email theradiantjester@gmail.com.
